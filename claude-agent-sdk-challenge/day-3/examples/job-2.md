# AI Product Analyst, Ledgerly (fictional sample job ad)

Remote (UK) · £45,000 to £52,000

Ledgerly makes accounting software for small businesses. You'll own the analytics behind
our new AI bookkeeping assistant.

## What you'll do
- Define and track the metrics that show whether the AI assistant is helping customers.
- Write SQL against our data warehouse (BigQuery) every day.
- Run A/B tests and present the results to product and leadership.
- Work with engineers on evaluation datasets for the assistant's answers.

## What we need
- 3+ years in product or data analytics.
- Strong SQL: window functions, CTEs, query performance.
- Experience designing and analysing A/B tests.
- Comfortable presenting to senior stakeholders.

## Nice to have
- Python for analysis (pandas).
- Experience in fintech or accounting.
- Has worked on an AI or ML product.
