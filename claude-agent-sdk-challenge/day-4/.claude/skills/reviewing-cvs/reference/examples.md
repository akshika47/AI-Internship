# Before and after

Each rewrite keeps every fact from the original line and adds none.

| Original CV line | Rewrite for an automation role |
|---|---|
| Responsible for weekly reporting across paid search, social and email | Owned weekly reporting across paid search, social and email for a £1.2m ad budget *(only if £1.2m is on the CV)* |
| Rebuilt the marketing dashboard in Looker Studio; cut the Monday reporting job from about 4 hours to 30 minutes | Cut the Monday reporting job from about 4 hours to 30 minutes by rebuilding the dashboard in Looker Studio |
| Helped onboard new starters | Onboarded new coordinators and wrote the team's process guide *(if both are on the CV)* |
| Worked on a chatbot project during my internship | Built a RAG chatbot over a 40-page company FAQ during the TAI Labs AI Internship |

## What not to do

| Rewrite | Problem |
|---|---|
| Increased efficiency by 40% through automation | 40% isn't on the CV: invented number |
| Spearheaded cross-functional synergies | Buzzwords, no result |
| Responsible for optimizing workflows | Duty phrase, US spelling, no result |
