# UK CV rules, with sources

Use this when you need the reasoning behind a rule or a rule not in SKILL.md.

## Contents
- Content: achievements, verbs, evidence
- Fit: tailoring to the job ad
- Format: length, layout, applicant tracking systems
- What to leave out

## Content

- **Achievements over duties.** Recruiters care what you achieved, not what your job
  description said. Source: Bright Network employer advice; Wikijob CV guide.
- **Active, strong verbs.** Use active verbs, not passive ones, and avoid weak ones such as
  "tried to" or "attempted to". Source: University of Nottingham Careers Service.
- **Evidence beats adjectives.** Avoid "hard-working", "team player", "passionate". Show it
  with a result instead. Source: Bright Network; BCS, "Does your CV sell you?".
- **Numbers make results concrete**, but only numbers that are true and on the CV.

## Fit

- **Mirror the job ad's language.** Use the employer's own words for skills you really have,
  starting with anything marked essential or required. Source: University of Manchester
  Careers Service, "Creating your CV".
- **Tailor, don't rewrite from scratch.** Reorder and sharpen what's relevant to this role.

## Format

- **Two pages at most** for most applicants. Source: Wikijob CV guide.
- **Simple layout.** Standard fonts and plain structure, so applicant tracking systems can
  read it. Two-column templates and skill bars often parse badly.
- **Short profile**: three or four sentences, specific to the kind of role.

## Leave out (UK)

- Photo, date of birth, nationality, marital status. Source: University of Nottingham Careers
  Service.
- "References available on request". It's assumed.
