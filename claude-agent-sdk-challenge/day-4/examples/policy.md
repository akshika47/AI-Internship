# Remote working policy, Fernhill Health (fictional sample document)

Last updated: March 2026

## Where you can work
Staff can work from home up to three days a week. Your manager must agree your home days in
advance, and they're recorded in the HR system.

Working from another country needs written approval from HR before you travel. Approval is
given for up to 10 working days in any 12-month period. You must stay within the UK's time zone
or two hours of it.

## Equipment
Fernhill provides a laptop and one monitor. You can claim up to £150 once for a desk chair,
with a receipt, within your first three months.

## Security
Patient data must never be opened on a personal device. Public Wi-Fi is only allowed with the
company VPN switched on.
