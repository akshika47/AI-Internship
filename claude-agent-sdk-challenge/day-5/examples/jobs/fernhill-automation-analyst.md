# Junior AI Automation Analyst, Fernhill Health (fictional sample job ad)

Hybrid, Manchester · £32,000 to £36,000

We're a 200-person digital health company. You'll join our operations team to find
repetitive work and automate it with AI tools.

## What you'll do
- Map how our support and scheduling teams work today and spot what to automate.
- Build automations with Python, Zapier and the Claude or OpenAI APIs.
- Measure time saved and report it to the operations lead every month.
- Train colleagues to use the tools you build.

## What we need
- Some Python, comfortable reading and changing scripts.
- Experience with a no-code automation tool (Zapier, Make or n8n).
- Clear writing for non-technical colleagues.
- Hands-on use of an LLM API in a project.

## Nice to have
- SQL.
- Experience in healthcare or another regulated industry.
- Has built an AI agent that uses tools.
