## Item 1A. Risk Factors

### Concentration of Cloud Hosting Revenue

A substantial portion of our revenue is derived from the Hosting Services segment, which accounted for 64% of total revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026. A loss of a significant hosting customer, a decline in demand for our hosting services, or a successful challenge by a competitor could materially adversely affect our business, results of operations, and financial condition.

### Dependence on Third-Party Data Centers

We lease data center capacity from third parties in nine metropolitan areas. Any disruption in the services provided by these data center operators, including failure to renew leases on commercially reasonable terms, could adversely affect our operations.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

### Overview

The third quarter of 2026 was defined by sustained growth in our core Hosting Services segment, offset in part by continued headwinds in Professional Services. Total revenue for the quarter was $643.3M, an increase of 27% from $507.1M in the comparable period a year ago.

### Results of Operations

The discussion below compares the three months ended September 30, 2026 with the three months ended September 30, 2025.

<!-- page: 47 -->

#### Hosting Services

Hosting Services segment revenue grew 34% year-over-year in the third quarter of 2026, driven by higher utilization across our enterprise customer base and the migration of two large accounts onto our dedicated hosting product line. Operating margin for the segment expanded 180 basis points to 29.4%.

#### Managed Platform

Managed Platform segment revenue grew 18% year-over-year, below our expectations. We attribute the shortfall to extended sales cycles among mid-market customers and a longer-than-anticipated ramp of the product features released in the second quarter.

#### Professional Services

Professional Services revenue declined 6% year-over-year, consistent with the company's announced transition away from custom integration engagements. We expect this segment to stabilize in the first half of 2027.

### Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and cash equivalents were $812.6M at September 30, 2026, compared to $701.4M at December 31, 2025. Operating cash flow for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $489.2M.
