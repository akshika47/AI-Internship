## Letter to Shareholders

2025 was the year NetBay became a hosting company first. We launched our dedicated hosting product line, renewed every one of our top 50 accounts, and made the hard decision to step away from custom integration work.

We enter 2026 with more contracted revenue than at any point in our history, a stronger balance sheet, and a clearer focus on the products our customers renew.

We continue to invest in cloud leadership, and remain committed to a customer-first strategy that we believe will drive sustained revenue growth and expanding margins over the long term.

## Item 1. Business

### Overview

NetBay provides hosting, managed platform, and professional services to enterprise and mid-market customers. We report revenue in three segments: Hosting Services, Managed Platform, and Professional Services.

### Facilities

We lease data center capacity from third parties in seven metropolitan areas in North America and Europe. We do not own any data center facilities.

<!-- page: 14 -->

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

### Concentration of Cloud Hosting Revenue

A substantial portion of our revenue is derived from the Hosting Services segment, which accounted for 61% of total revenue for the year ended December 31, 2025. A loss of a significant hosting customer, a decline in demand for our hosting services, or a successful challenge by a competitor could materially adversely affect our business, results of operations, and financial condition.

### Customer Concentration

Our ten largest customers accounted for 23% of total revenue for the year ended December 31, 2025. No single customer accounted for more than 10% of total revenue.

### Dependence on Third-Party Data Centers

We lease data center capacity from third parties in seven metropolitan areas. Any disruption in the services provided by these data center operators, including failure to renew leases on commercially reasonable terms, could adversely affect our operations.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

### Overview

Total revenue for fiscal 2025 was $1,995.3M, an increase of 19% from $1,676.7M in fiscal 2024. Growth was led by Hosting Services, partly offset by the planned decline in Professional Services.

<!-- page: 42 -->

### Results of Operations

#### Hosting Services

Hosting Services segment revenue grew 22% year-over-year in fiscal 2025, driven by renewal strength in the top 50 customer accounts and the launch of our dedicated hosting product line in the third quarter. Operating margin for the segment was 27.2%.

#### Managed Platform

Managed Platform segment revenue grew 16% year-over-year in fiscal 2025, driven by new mid-market customers.

#### Professional Services

Professional Services revenue declined 9% year-over-year. In the second quarter of 2025 we announced a transition away from custom integration engagements, which we expect to complete during 2026.

<!-- page: 47 -->

### Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and cash equivalents were $701.4M at December 31, 2025, compared to $566.0M at December 31, 2024. Operating cash flow for fiscal 2025 was $598.7M.
