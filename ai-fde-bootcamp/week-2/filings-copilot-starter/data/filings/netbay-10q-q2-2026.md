## Item 1A. Risk Factors

### Concentration of Cloud Hosting Revenue

A substantial portion of our revenue is derived from the Hosting Services segment, which accounted for 63% of total revenue for the three months ended June 30, 2026. A loss of a significant hosting customer, a decline in demand for our hosting services, or a successful challenge by a competitor could materially adversely affect our business, results of operations, and financial condition.

### Dependence on Third-Party Data Centers

We lease data center capacity from third parties in eight metropolitan areas. Any disruption in the services provided by these data center operators, including failure to renew leases on commercially reasonable terms, could adversely affect our operations.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

### Overview

The second quarter of 2026 continued the momentum in our Hosting Services segment, supported by the dedicated hosting product line launched in 2025. Total revenue for the quarter was $611.8M, an increase of 25% from $489.4M in the comparable period a year ago.

### Results of Operations

The discussion below compares the three months ended June 30, 2026 with the three months ended June 30, 2025.

<!-- page: 44 -->

#### Hosting Services

Hosting Services segment revenue grew 31% year-over-year in the second quarter of 2026, driven by continued adoption of our dedicated hosting product line and higher utilization among existing enterprise customers. Operating margin for the segment expanded 120 basis points to 28.1%.

#### Managed Platform

Managed Platform segment revenue grew 21% year-over-year. During the quarter we released new workload automation features for Managed Platform customers, which we expect to contribute to revenue in the second half of 2026.

#### Professional Services

Professional Services revenue declined 4% year-over-year, consistent with the company's announced transition away from custom integration engagements.

### Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and cash equivalents were $758.3M at June 30, 2026, compared to $701.4M at December 31, 2025. Operating cash flow for the six months ended June 30, 2026 was $311.5M.
