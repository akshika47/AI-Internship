## NetBay Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

Total revenue of $644.1M, up 27% year over year. Hosting Services revenue up 34% year over year.

"Hosting Services carried the quarter again, and our customers keep moving more of their workloads onto dedicated hosting," said the Chief Executive Officer of NetBay.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## Revenue Summary

Revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026:

Hosting Services: $413.1M
Managed Platform: $186.1M
Professional Services: $44.9M
Total: $644.1M

Total revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $644.1M, compared to $507.1M in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 27%.
