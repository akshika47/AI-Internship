# Practice inbox: Saltline Bike & Kayak Hire

A fictional inbox you can use instead of your real one for the whole challenge. Every business, person, email
address and town in it is made up, so you can upload it anywhere, break things and start again.

## The business

- **Saltline Bike & Kayak Hire**, a small family business on the harbour in the seaside town of Port Mango.
- **Rosa Pereira** owns it. **Dev** is the mechanic. **Lina** works the front desk part-time.
- It's mid-November 2026, low season: open weekends only since 1 November, prices in euros.

Today in this inbox is **Monday 16 November 2026**, a closed day. The emails run from Monday 9 to Monday 16
November.

## What's here

| File | What it is | Give it to Claude? |
|---|---|---|
| `faq.md` | Rosa's facts and policies: hours, prices, booking, refunds, weather, safety, and a "Never promise" list | Yes |
| `emails/` | 36 emails, one file each, `01-...md` to `36-...md`, oldest first | Yes |
| `inbox-all.md` | The same 36 emails in one file, for uploading to a chat | Yes |

The mix is what a real small-business inbox looks like in a quiet week: routine questions, booking requests
(some missing details), cancellations and refund requests, complaints, supplier invoices and a receipt, reviews,
newsletters, a job application, an email in Portuguese, a chaser, a duplicate, one email that could be read two
ways, and one safety report that can't wait.

## How to use it

**In a Claude chat (web or desktop):** upload `inbox-all.md` and `faq.md`, then give Claude your job.

**In Claude Code or Cowork:** point Claude at this folder, or at the `emails/` folder and `faq.md`.

**Afterwards:** compare what Claude did with the answer key, which lives in
[`../day-3/answer-key.md`](../day-3/answer-key.md), outside this folder, so Claude can't read the answers. Count how many of the 36 it got right, and look hardest
at the safety report, the refund requests and the email in Portuguese.

## Jobs it suits

- **Inbox desk** (the worked example): one log row per email, reply drafts from the FAQ, money, complaints and
  safety flagged for Rosa, nothing sent.
- **Receipts and invoices to a sheet**: three supplier emails carry amounts, invoice numbers and due dates, and
  a few others mention money that isn't a cost.
- **Booking requests to a calendar list**: a dozen emails name dates, some on closed days, some cancelled, some
  missing details.
- **Weekly summary**: a handful of themes run through several emails each, plus a few things only one customer
  said.

The answer key has the expected result for each of these jobs.
