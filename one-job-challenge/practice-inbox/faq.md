# Saltline Bike & Kayak Hire: facts and policies

Source: Saltline Bike & Kayak Hire, owner's FAQ for replies
Context: Fictional sample data for the One Job challenge. The business, town and people are made up.

I'm Rosa Pereira and I run Saltline with my family. Every reply to a customer uses only what is in this file.
If a question isn't answered here, don't guess: pass it to me.

## Who we are

- Saltline Bike & Kayak Hire, 4 Harbour Row, Port Mango. Email: hello@saltline.example
- Rosa (me): owner. Money, refunds, complaints, group prices and anything unusual come to me.
- Dev: mechanic. Every repair, and every fault report on a bike or kayak.
- Lina: front desk on Saturdays and Sundays. She keeps the booking sheet and confirms bookings.
- In low season Lina and I answer email on Saturdays and Sundays, so a weekday email can wait a few days.
- Emails in Portuguese get a reply in Portuguese (I speak it). Every other language gets a reply in English.

## Opening hours

- Low season, 1 November to 31 March: **Saturday and Sunday, 10:00 to 16:00. Closed Monday to Friday.**
- Last hire goes out at 14:30. Everything comes back by 16:00.
- Christmas closure: closed from Monday 21 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027. Last open days before it are
  Saturday 19 and Sunday 20 December. We reopen on Saturday 9 January 2027.
- High season, 1 April to 31 October: every day, 09:00 to 18:00.
- Weekday hire in low season only for groups of 8 or more, booked at least 7 days ahead, and only if I agree.

## Prices (euros, low season, VAT included)

| What | Per hour | Per day or half day | Deposit |
|---|---|---|---|
| City bike | 8 | 25 per day | 50 |
| E-bike (2 hour minimum) | 15 | 45 per day | 150 |
| Kids' bike (about age 6 to 12) | 5 | 15 per day | 30 |
| Single kayak | 15 | 35 per half day (3 hours) | 50 |
| Double kayak | 22 | 50 per half day (3 hours) | 50 |

- A "day" is our opening hours that day. If the hours add up to more than the day price, we charge the day price.
- Kayaks go out by the hour or the half day only. No full-day kayak hire in low season.
- Child seat (for children up to 22 kg, roughly age 1 to 4): 5 per day. Child trailer (two children, up to
  22 kg each): 10 per day. We have 4 seats and 1 trailer, so book them.
- Free with every hire: helmets in every size from toddler up, a lock, life jackets for kayaks, a repair kit.
- Deposits are held on a card or paid in cash, and returned at drop-off once Dev or Lina has checked the bike.
- Gift vouchers: 25 or 50 euros, valid 12 months. Buy in the shop, or by bank transfer: I send the bank details
  and email the voucher when the money arrives. Vouchers can't be refunded or swapped for cash. I can extend
  one once.

## Our fleet

20 city bikes, 6 e-bikes (numbered E1 to E6), 8 kids' bikes, 6 single kayaks, 4 double kayaks.
An e-bike battery lasts about 50 to 60 km on the flat. A full charge does the ridge loop with plenty to spare.

## Booking

- Book by email. Tell us the date, start time, number of people, what you'd like, each rider's height (for the
  frame size) and the age of any child.
- **A booking is only confirmed when Lina or I reply with the word "confirmed".** Replies to new requests say we'll
  check availability. They never say the booking is done.
- City bikes: walk-ins welcome. E-bikes, kayaks, child seats and the trailer must be booked.
- Up to 5 people: no prepayment, pay when you pick up.
- Groups of 6 or more: 20% of the total paid in advance by bank transfer to hold the booking.
- Groups of 10 or more: I work out the price myself. Replies don't quote a group price.

## Cancellations and refunds

- No prepayment: cancel any time, free. We just ask for 24 hours' notice.
- Prepaid groups: cancel more than 7 days before and the prepayment is refunded. Cancel 7 days or less before
  and the prepayment is kept, or moved once to another date.
- **Every refund, full or partial, is decided by me.** Replies never say a refund is approved and never name an
  amount going back.

## Weather

- Bikes go out in rain. We only stop bike hire for an official storm warning.
- Kayaks don't go out if the wind is over 25 km/h or there is any weather warning. I decide by 08:00 on the day
  and email everyone booked.
- If we cancel for weather, you choose: a free move to another date, or your prepayment back. I handle these.
- If you choose not to come because of rain, the normal cancellation rules apply.

## Children and safety

- Helmets are compulsory for under-16s and free for everyone.
- E-bikes: riders aged 16 and over only.
- Kayaks: 16 and over can paddle alone. Ages 8 to 15 go in a double kayak with an adult. Under 8: no kayaks.
- Life jackets are compulsory on the water. Our smallest fits children from 25 kg.
- Every kayak hire starts with a 10-minute safety briefing on the beach.
- **Any report of a fault on a hired bike or kayak (brakes, battery, steering, a leak) comes to me and Dev the same
  day, even on a closed day.** That bike or kayak stays off hire until Dev has checked it.

## Routes

- Coastal path to Lighthouse Point: 12 km there and back, flat, good with children.
- Ridge loop: 25 km, a long climb out of town and a steep descent at the end. E-bike recommended. Adults only.

## Parking and getting here

- No parking at the shop. Use the harbour car park on Quay Street, 2 minutes' walk away.
- The harbour car park is free from 1 November to 31 March, and 1.50 an hour from April to October.
- Bus 3 from Port Mango station stops on Harbour Row.

## Payment

- Card (debit or credit) or cash in euros. Bank transfer for group prepayments and gift vouchers.
- No other currencies and no cheques.
- Receipts on request. For a company invoice, send the company name, address and VAT number.

## Damage, breakdowns and loss

- Normal wear and punctures: free.
- Breakdown on the route: ring the shop number on your hire slip. On open days Dev brings a replacement within
  5 km of the shop.
- Damage beyond normal wear: Dev checks it and the repair cost comes out of the deposit. If it costs more than
  the deposit, I get in touch.
- Lost or stolen bike: the deposit is kept and I get in touch about the rest.

## Things we don't do

No guided tours, no lessons, no overnight hire, no delivery to hotels.

## Never promise

- A discount, free extra, price match or special deal of any kind.
- A refund, a refund amount, or compensation for fuel, travel, meals or clothing.
- That a booking is confirmed, or that a date or a particular bike is available.
- Opening on a day or at a time not listed above.
- That a bike or kayak someone reported is safe or has been fixed.
- A job, a trial shift or an interview.
- Anything this file doesn't cover. Say I'll get back to them.
