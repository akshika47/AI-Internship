Recompete Readiness Map

A fixed-scope first step for a firm whose contract is ending and whose proposal team is small. One price, one map, one recommended next step. Price: [J2i to set].

Why this offer exists

Two fictional prospects share the problem. Harborline Systems Group: the biggest task order ends in March (about 40 percent of revenue) and "our proposal team is three people and exhausted" (calls/discovery-call.md, inbound/linkedin_dms.md). Bluefield Federal Partners: "recompete in Q1" (inbound/conference_scans.csv), but its team size is not stated [VERIFY].

Who it is for

  • A veteran-owned or government-contracting firm of about 50 to 500 staff (ASSUMPTION), or a VA-approved vendor.
  • A contract or task order that will be re-competed, with a known or expected solicitation date.
  • A small proposal team: a capture or proposal lead doing most of the work by hand.
  • Buyer: the COO, CEO or owner. User: the capture and proposal lead. Approver: whoever signs the spend, plus IT security for any tool (ASSUMPTION until J2i confirms).
  • Not for: resellers of hardware or software, vendors, recruiters, students, or a firm with no dated recompete.

What the client gets

  1. Recompete timeline: contract end, option years, draft and final solicitation, due date, each with its source. Unknown dates marked [VERIFY].
  2. Proposal workload map: where the hours go today (reading the solicitation, the compliance matrix, hunting past performance, chasing experts), in the client's own numbers. Missing numbers read ESTIMATE NEEDED.
  3. Past performance inventory: where the write-ups live and what is missing.
  4. Tool and data check: tools in use, which data is controlled, and what the IT security lead must approve.
  5. One-page AI Opportunity Map: top three opportunities for this recompete and exactly one recommended first step.
  6. Dated readiness plan: what has to be done before the draft solicitation, with owners named by title.

Not included

Writing the proposal, building any tool, a promise to win, or legal advice. Anything extra is a change order (knowledge/index.md lists the template).

How long it takes

About two weeks from kickoff [TO CONFIRM], faster when the inputs arrive on day one.

  1. Kickoff call with the COO and the capture and proposal lead.
  2. Client sends dates, the last proposals' hour counts, the document locations and the tool list. No controlled content is needed: the map uses counts, dates and locations, not the contents of the files.
  3. Working session with the capture and proposal lead, and the IT security lead for the tool check.
  4. Review: a named J2i person reviews the draft before the client sees it. Nothing invented, every number from the client or ESTIMATE NEEDED.
  5. Handover of the map and plan.

Start no later than [J2i to set] weeks before the expected draft solicitation.

How it leads into the discovery sprint

  • The sprint ends in a one-page AI Opportunity Map within 24 hours of the discovery call. This offer does the recompete homework first, so the call starts with a point of view (the method in knowledge/method/session-1-prompts-and-method.txt).
  • At handover the client chooses: (a) run the discovery sprint with the wider team to test the one recommended first step, (b) move straight to implementation of that step with the build partner, or (c) stop and keep the map. The map is theirs either way.
  • If the client goes ahead, training follows so their own team uses it on real proposals.
  • Credit toward the sprint: [J2i to set] whether any of the fee is credited, and for how many days.

Price logic

  • Priced against what is at stake, not against hours: the client states the share of revenue riding on the contract (Harborline said about 40 percent). The revenue figure itself is ESTIMATE NEEDED for any new prospect.
  • Floor: the fee has to cover J2i's time on the work at [J2i to set]. J2i's cost and rates are not in the files.
  • Payment terms to start from: 50 percent on signature, 50 percent on completion, invoices payable in 14 days, as in knowledge/templates/consulting-proposal.txt. [TO CONFIRM] for this offer.
  • Out-of-scope work is a change order at a day rate of [J2i to set].
