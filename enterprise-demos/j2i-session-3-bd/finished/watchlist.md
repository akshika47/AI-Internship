## Rules changing for these buyers this year

Both rules come from two web searches (secondary sources only, no official page opened). The official pages below are from memory and not verified, so each is an ASSUMPTION until opened.

### 1. CMMC (cybersecurity certification for defence contractors)
- What: DFARS clause 252.204-7021 took effect on 2025-11-10. It lets contracting officers require a CMMC level as a condition of award, and the requirement flows down to subcontractors.
- Conflict: most sources say Phase 2 (mandatory third-party certification for most contracts that handle CUI) starts 2026-11-10. One source (Red River) says the Department of War suspended Phase 2 on 2026-07-13 for a 60-day review. I could not confirm that suspension from an official page. ASSUMPTION: status is unclear. Check before telling a client either way.
- Official page: ASSUMPTION: https://dodcio.defense.gov/cmmc/ (DoD CIO CMMC page). Not opened. A related page that did appear in the results is https://www.dla.mil/Small-Business/Resources/Cybersecurity-Resources/.
- Why it matters to J2i: it connects to house rule 1 (client data only in approved tools). Contractors with CUI will ask where their data goes.

### 2. Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (rewrite of the Federal Acquisition Regulation)
- What: a government-wide rewrite begun by Executive Order 14275 (April 2025). Agencies first applied it through temporary class deviations. On 2026-06-23 the FAR Council published four proposed rules covering 20 FAR parts. Comments closed 2026-07-23. Twelve proposed rules in all are planned. A firm expects the process to finish by end of 2026, which is not confirmed.
- Result for buyers: a contract may sit under the original FAR, the deviation version or the final overhauled FAR.
- Official page: ASSUMPTION: https://www.acquisition.gov/far-overhaul. Not opened.
- Why it matters to J2i: buyers may be unsure of the rules and ask for help. That is an opening, but J2i must not give legal or compliance advice (ASSUMPTION: outside J2i's stated services).
