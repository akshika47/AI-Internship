How it was decided

method
  • Each contract’s description was read by keyword rules and put in one of three groups: services, resale, or unclear. ASSUMPTION: the rules are mine, not J2i’s, and are not a full review.
  • Resale means licences, subscriptions, named software or hardware products, renewals, or equipment purchases. Engineering, support, management, analysis and training services count as services, even when software is named.
  • A firm is flagged when more than half of its ending contracts are resale. Half exactly is not flagged.
  • 66 of 535 contracts have descriptions too thin to tell, such as “TASK ORDER #5”. They count as not resale. Firms in the table with unclear ones: Grant Leading, Appddictionstudio, Stellar, Oddcore, DecisionPoint, Goldpath, C5MI.
  • I read the descriptions behind every firm in the table and checked them against this rule. The first rule version wrongly flagged AD Hoc Research; that is fixed.
