Two rules changing for these buyers this year

Both affect government contractors. They shape what a buyer must spend on and what they will want AI to help with.

Revolutionary FAR Overhaul

Rule 1

The Federal Acquisition Regulation is being rewritten. Formal rulemaking began on 23 June 2026 with four proposed rules covering 20 FAR parts; 12 are planned to cover the whole FAR. Comments on the first four closed 23 July 2026. The target to finish by the end of 2026 is described by one source as ambitious.
