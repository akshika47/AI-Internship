1. Before launch

weeks 1 to 2 · ASSUMPTION

Goal

The offer can be sold: price, duration and terms settled, a list of firms with a dated recompete, and a message that answers "nothing invented".

One number to track

6 to 0 open items on offer.html: price, duration, start lead time, sprint credit, payment terms, and whether Bluefield has a small proposal team.

Channels

  • Internal review by a named person (every output is a draft until then).

  • Apollo and Clay lookups only after the credit cost is stated and approved.

2. Launch

weeks 3 to 8 · ASSUMPTION

Goal

Get discovery calls with firms whose contract is ending and whose proposal team is small, and make the first offers.

One number to track

Discovery calls held per week. Base need: about 2.6 a week if the goal is read as 31 Dec 2026 (see below).

Channels

  • Outreach to the call-first list, drafted with AI and sent by a person. Nobody is added to a sequence.
  • The founder's LinkedIn posts. Harborline's DM opened with "Saw your post on AI for proposal teams" (fictional).
  • Conference follow-ups: the fictional VetBiz scans are 7 of the 14 records in inbound/.
  • Marketplace requests and warm introductions.

3. After launch

week 9 on · ASSUMPTION

Goal

Turn calls into signed Maps, deliver each on time, and learn why firms say no.

One number to track

Maps delivered, out of ten. Also watch signed Maps per discovery call: the funnel below assumes 40 percent.

Channels

  • win-loss.html: log every outcome, reason and alternative named.
  • Referral asks to clients after delivery, with their permission.
  • The next paid step for each client: discovery sprint, implementation, training.
  • 90-day follow-up emails to parked firms, sent by a person.
