GovDash

2026-10-12

What they say now

“AI infrastructure to win government contracts.” One platform for discovery, capture, pricing, proposals and contracts. Source, 14 Sep 2026

Where they are strong

  • They claim one system from finding bids to managing contracts, and “300+ teams”. Source
  • They claim “FedRAMP Moderate Equivalent & Ready” and a self-hosted option. Not checked by J2i. Source

Where J2i fits

ASSUMPTION: a buyer whose problem is wider than proposals, or who needs their own staff trained on AI. J2i sells discovery, implementation and training. Source: J2i context

Three questions for the buyer

  1. Which step of your bid process costs you the most time today, and who owns it?
  2. Who would use it every day, and what changes in their week?
  3. Where does your past performance and proposal material live now?

Likely objections

  • “It already does AI proposals.” ASSUMPTION: it may be the right choice if proposals are your only need. If other work is in scope, J2i’s discovery sprint ends in an AI Opportunity Map. Source: J2i context
