# J2i context (public-safe version for the live demo)

Source: TAI Labs programme notes and J2i's Session 1 kit. No pricing, contracts or partner terms here.

## Who J2i is
- J2 Intelligence Group: an AI-native consultancy, veteran-led, with a military and commercial background.
- Core team: a founder who runs strategy and operations, a business development lead, a CTO focused on AI
  orchestration and local models, plus about ten consultants (data, healthcare, AI engineering, logistics, UX).
- J2i owns the client relationship, product management and data storytelling. A build partner does the
  engineering on implementation work.

## What J2i sells
- Discovery sprint: hypothesis-led discovery, ending in an AI Opportunity Map within 24 hours of the call.
- AI implementation with the build partner, then ongoing support.
- Training and enablement so a client's own team uses AI on real work.
- A low-cost entry offer is wanted for faster cash flow (an Opportunity Map or workshop).

## Who J2i wants to sell to (current direction)
- Mid-sized and larger veteran-owned businesses, including VA-approved vendors and government contractors.
- Small firms tend to "kick the tires"; larger ones give steadier work.
- Also commercial clients, to build a track record (for example a healthcare clinic prospect earlier this year).

## How J2i finds work today
- Warm LinkedIn messages and inbound requests; a work marketplace; local network; monthly AI workshops;
  conferences with an on-site offer. No CRM in use yet.
- Goal: about ten clients in the first year, and a first contract soon.

## J2i's method (already in use)
- Qualification gate, four signals scored 0 or 1 each: budget signal, decision authority, AI readiness,
  problem clarity. 3-4 = discovery sprint; 1-2 = fit call or nurture; 0 = helpful email and 90 day follow up.
- Discovery: P1 research brief before the call, SPIN questions on the call, P3 transcript extraction and
  P4 Opportunity Map after it. Never solution on the call. One commitment only: the 24 hour Map.
- Data rules: client data only in approved tools; every AI output is a draft until a named person reviews it;
  AI never invents a number (write ESTIMATE NEEDED); answer honestly if a client asks how we work.
