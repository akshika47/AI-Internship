“Why not just buy the software?”

Prepared for a COO at a veteran-owned contractor who is also looking at Sweetspot. Everything below comes from the Sweetspot card in battlecards.html and nothing else. The card line each point comes from is named beside it.

30-second answer

Say this

“Sweetspot may be exactly right for what it does. It says it brings capture, pipeline management and proposal writing into one platform, and a team that can set it up alone may not need more. J2i offers discovery and training. Where we fit is if you want help deciding what to do with AI, with a person working through it with your team. So my question is: what would you want a person to help with that software wouldn’t?”

About 75 words, roughly 30 seconds spoken. ASSUMPTION: the timing is my estimate, not tested aloud.
