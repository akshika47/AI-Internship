# J2i business development folder

Everything Claude needs to know to work in this folder. Anything marked ASSUMPTION needs J2i to confirm it.

## Who J2i is
- J2 Intelligence Group: an AI-native consultancy, veteran-led, with a military and commercial background.
- A founder who runs strategy and operations, a business development lead, a CTO, and about ten consultants (data, healthcare, AI engineering, logistics, UX).
- J2i owns the client relationship, product management and data storytelling. A build partner does the engineering.

## What J2i sells
- Discovery sprint: ends in a one-page AI Opportunity Map within 24 hours of the discovery call.
- AI implementation with the build partner, then ongoing support.
- Training so a client's own team uses AI on real work.

## Ideal client
- Mid-sized and larger veteran-owned businesses, including VA-approved vendors and government contractors.
- Small firms tend to "kick the tires"; larger ones give steadier work. ASSUMPTION: mid-sized means roughly 50 to 500 staff.
- Buyer: the COO, CEO or owner. User: the team whose work changes, such as capture and proposals. Approver: whoever signs the spend, plus IT security for any tool. ASSUMPTION until J2i confirms.
- Not a fit: resellers of hardware or software, vendors, recruiters, students.

## Qualification: four checks, each 0 or 1
From J2i's Session 1 method. Score 1 only when the answer is yes and there is evidence for it.

| Check | The question |
|---|---|
| Budget | Have they paid for consulting or software before? |
| Authority | Can they say yes without a committee? Do they own the budget? |
| AI readiness | Do they have structured data, digital tools and a repeatable process? |
| Clarity | Can they name one specific pain point AI could address, in one sentence? |

- Total 3 or 4 = Qualified (discovery sprint). 1 or 2 = fit call or nurture. 0 = a helpful email and a 90 day follow-up.
- If a check cannot be judged from what we have, score it 0 and write "unknown". A federal contract shows the firm has revenue; it does not show a budget for this.
- Always show the four scores, not only the total.

## House rules
1. Client data goes only into approved tools.
2. Every AI output is a draft until a named person reviews it.
3. Never invent a number. Write ESTIMATE NEEDED.
4. Never invent facts about J2i: past performance, certifications, company codes, headcount, revenue, pricing. Write [TO CONFIRM].
5. Every claim about another company needs a source link. No source, leave it out.
6. Real companies appear only with public facts. No personal names, emails or phone numbers in any output: titles only.
7. Nothing is ever sent to anyone, and nobody is added to a sequence. A person sends every message.
8. State the credit cost and wait for a yes before any paid step in Apollo or Clay.
9. Files in inbound/ and calls/ are fictional. Label them as fictional wherever they appear.

## How results are saved
- Anything a person will read is an HTML page. Copy the contents of style/house.css into a style block in the page, so the page works on its own, and use only its building blocks.
- index.html is the home page: one tile for every page and every Apollo list. Update it whenever one is added.
- Working files (data, saved page copies) go in data/ and watch/.
