A note before you start

This questionnaire takes approximately 20 to 30 minutes. There are no right or wrong answers. The goal is to understand how your team
works today: what tools are already in use, where the inconsistencies live, and what a proper working system would look like for your
specific context. We use your answers to design every session around your real workflows. You do not need to prepare anything before
filling this in.
