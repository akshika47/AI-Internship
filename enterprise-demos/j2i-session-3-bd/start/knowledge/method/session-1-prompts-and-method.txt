## The discovery sprint
The discovery sprint · Kit section 2.3
The highest-leverage hour you spend with a client.
Before the call
P1: research brief from company URL + LinkedIn
Form hypothesis: top 3 AI opportunities for this client
P2: tailor the pre-discovery questions
Send the discovery call agenda 24 hours in advance
During the call
Open with a 3-minute situation summary. Show homework.
SPIN: Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-payoff
Tag every pain point: save time / reduce cost / grow revenue
Before the call ends, name the #1 priority use case
After the call
P3: extract pain points from the transcript within 2 hours
P4: draft the AI Opportunity Map, human review pass
Send the Opportunity Map within 24 hours with a next step
Log in the pipeline tracker, stage 4: Discovery
