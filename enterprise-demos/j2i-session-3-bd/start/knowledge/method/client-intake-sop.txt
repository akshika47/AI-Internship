Overview

This SOP covers every step from the moment a client signs the proposal through to the end of Session 1.

Total window: approximately 3 weeks from signature to Session 1.




Step 1: Confirm signed proposal received

Owner: Snapdrum (programme lead)
Timing: Same day as signature

        Confirm the signed proposal or SOW is received and filed

        Note the client legal entity name, billing contact, and nominated programme contact

        Log the engagement in the pipeline tracker with status: Signed

        Check payment terms: is this 50/50 or a custom split?

Handover note: Pass the signed document and the client's key contacts to the delivery lead within 24 hours.




Step 2: Issue the SOW for countersignature (if not already signed)

Owner: Snapdrum (programme lead)
Timing: Within 1 business day of verbal or proposal confirmation

        Open the SOW template and fill in the client legal entity name, programme details, agreed fee, and dates

        Review: no named deliverable counts, no outcome percentages, fees match the closed deal exactly

        Send the SOW to the client's signing authority with a short covering note

        Set a 3-day follow-up reminder if no countersignature received

Do not proceed to Step 3 until the SOW is countersigned.




Step 3: Issue the deposit invoice

Owner: Snapdrum (finance / programme lead)
Timing: Within 1 business day of SOW countersignature

        Open the invoice template

        Fill in: invoice number (INV-[YYYY]-[XXX]), invoice date, due date (14 days), SOW reference, client billing details, deposit amount
        (50% of total fee)

        Send to the client's billing contact with the engagement letter

        Log invoice as Issued in the billing tracker

Step 4: Send the engagement letter

Owner: Snapdrum (programme lead)
Timing: Same day as deposit invoice

        Open the engagement letter template

        Fill in: client contact name, SOW date, programme start window, next steps, deposit amount, nominated contact details

        Send from [programme email] (or a named team member's address per agreed comms approach)

        Attach or reference: SOW (if not already sent), invoice




Step 5: Send the discovery questionnaire

Owner: Snapdrum (programme lead / delivery lead)
Timing: Within 2 business days of engagement letter

        Open the discovery questionnaire template

        Confirm: no changes needed based on what we already know about this client

        Send to the nominated programme contact with a clear return deadline (2 weeks before Session 1)

        Include a short note explaining why the questions matter and what we will do with the answers




Step 6: Confirm deposit payment received

Owner: Snapdrum (finance / programme lead)
Timing: By the due date on the deposit invoice

        Confirm payment has cleared

        Update billing tracker: Deposit Received

        If payment is late (past 14 days), send a polite payment reminder

        Do not schedule sessions until deposit is confirmed (unless an exception is agreed with the programme lead)




Step 7: Review the discovery questionnaire and build the tool audit

Owner: Delivery lead (Aki)
Timing: Within 2 business days of receiving the completed questionnaire

        Read the questionnaire in full

        Build a simple "tool audit" from Section B: list every person, every tool they use, how often, and what for

        Note the gap: what tools are in use versus what standard the team claims to have

        Highlight: which workflows the client wants to prioritise, any platform restrictions, any confidentiality constraints

        Note their answer to "what would a working system look like" (Section A, Q6) and "what would Monday morning look like" (Section C,
        Q6)

        Flag any gaps or unusual requests to the programme lead for a quick call with the client if needed

        Draft the session adaptation notes: which examples and exercises to use for each session, adapted to the client's specific workflows
        and pain points

Step 8: Build and confirm the session schedule

Owner: Programme lead (in collaboration with client's nominated contact)
Timing: Within 1 week of receiving the discovery questionnaire

        Propose a 6-week session schedule (one session per week, consistent day and time)

        Confirm the schedule with the client's nominated contact in writing

        Send calendar invites with Zoom links to all participants

        Confirm: participants list is correct and invites have been accepted

        Log confirmed dates in the programme tracker




Step 9: Prepare and send pre-programme materials

Owner: Delivery lead (Aki) + programme lead
Timing: No later than 3 business days before Session 1

        Confirm: discovery adaptation notes are incorporated into Session 1 materials

        Send to participants: pre-session prep note (what to expect, what to bring to Session 1, any light reading if applicable)

        Confirm: Zoom link is correct and working

        Confirm: recordings process is set up (Zoom auto-record to cloud, or agreed equivalent)

        Confirm: materials folder (shared drive / Notion / agreed channel) is created and accessible to the client's nominated contact




Step 10: Deliver Session 1

Owner: Delivery lead (Aki)
Timing: Confirmed session date

        Run the kickoff agenda (see 09 Kickoff Agenda) for the first 20 minutes

        Confirm participants are all present before diving into content

        Record the session

        Deliver the session recording and materials within 24 hours

        Send a short post-session note: what was covered, what to do before Session 2, any follow-up questions

        Update programme tracker: Session 1 delivered

After Session 1: Update the session plan if any adaptation is needed based on the group's level and responses.




Escalation

If any step is blocked (unsigned SOW after 5 days, late deposit, missing questionnaire, participant no-shows), escalate to the programme lead
immediately. Do not let a block sit for more than 2 business days without a decision on next steps.
