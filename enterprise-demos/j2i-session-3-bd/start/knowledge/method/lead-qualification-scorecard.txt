Lead Qualification Scorecard
A short, honest read on whether a new client opportunity is worth your discovery time. Score each line from 0 to 3 using the
guide beside it. Total it. The band at the bottom tells you what to do next. Five minutes here saves you a wasted fortnight later.



A scorecard's job is to make “no” cheap. The fastest way to lose a quarter is to pour discovery into a deal that was never
going to close. Score what is true today, not what you hope becomes true after one good call.

0 = not there at all · 1 = early · 2 = promising · 3 = a strong, evidenced signal.

Stop and re-think, whatever the score
Any one of these outranks a good total. A high score with a red flag is still a risk.
