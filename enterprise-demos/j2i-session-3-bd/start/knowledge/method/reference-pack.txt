4. Consulting glossary

Plain definitions for terms that come up across the toolkit.


    TERM                                         MEANS


    Scope                                        What is, and is not, included in the work


    Deliverable                                  A specific thing the client receives


    Engagement                                   One complete piece of client work, start to finish


    SOW                                          Statement of Work: the contract that defines the engagement


    Change order                                 A signed agreement to add or alter scope


    Discovery                                    The early phase of understanding how a client works


    Kickoff                                      The first working session that starts delivery


    Handover                                     Passing work, access and context to the client or to a colleague


    Sponsor                                      The senior person on the client side who owns the relationship and the budget
