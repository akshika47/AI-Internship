Why we are meeting

This call is for us to listen, not to pitch. The better we understand how your team actually works today, the warts and the wins, the more useful
everything after this becomes. Come with your real problems, not a tidy summary. There is nothing you can say here that makes us think less
of you.
Forty-five minutes is enough if we stay honest and skip the throat-clearing.

What to bring

You do not need to prepare slides. Just come ready to talk about:
◆ One workflow that eats more time than it should.
◆ Who actually does that work, day to day.
◆ Anything that is off the table (a tool you cannot use, data you cannot share).



What happens after

◆ Within two business days we send a short written recap of what we heard.
◆ If it is a fit, the recap comes with a Cost Brief and proposed dates.
◆ If it is not a fit, we will say so plainly, and point you somewhere better if we can.
