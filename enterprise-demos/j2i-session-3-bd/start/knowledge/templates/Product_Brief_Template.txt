The client-facing document
One page. Five sections. Sign before build.
Business language, no technology. Every section answers a question the sponsor needs to be able to defend in six months. If a sentence would embarrass them in a board meeting, rewrite it.
Client / engagement
Sponsor (name, role)
Consultant lead
01
Problem
What the person does today, how long it takes, how often, at what cost. In the client's own words and units. No mention of AI.
e.g. Intake coordinators receive 120 unstructured referrals a week and assign each to a clinician within 48 hours. Average time-to-triage is 34 hours. Rework rate on assignments is 18%.
02
User
The exact human whose next two hours change. Not the beneficiary further downstream. If you cannot name the team of three or five, you have the wrong section here.
e.g. The three-person intake team. Not clinicians. Not patients.
03
Success
The number the sponsor is willing to be held to. In client units. Time-to-X, throughput, error rate, cost per case, capacity per FTE. One primary metric, up to two secondary.
e.g. Time-to-triage under 12 hours on 90% of referrals. Rework rate below 8%. Intake capacity per FTE up 40% by month three.
04
Non-goals
What this project explicitly does not do. The most important section of the brief. Reduces scope creep to a written argument, not a fight.
e.g. Does not diagnose. Does not communicate with patients. Does not touch the risk-flagging workstream. Does not replace clinical sign-off.
05
Guardrails
The non-negotiable behaviours: what must be cited, what must be reviewed, what must be logged, what must never happen. The lines that stay when everything else is up for iteration.
e.g. Every assignment cites the policy sections used. Every urgent classification triggers a named human review inside one hour. Nothing leaves the system without a clinician's initials.
Also required before build.  The AI spec (engineering document), the compliance checklist (six boxes signed), and the client-sensitive information one-pager. Together they are what the handoff pack references.
