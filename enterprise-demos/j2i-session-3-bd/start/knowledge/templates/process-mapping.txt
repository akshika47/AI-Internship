Processes to map first

If you are not sure where to start, pick the process that is most painful or most frequent:

1        Post-meeting notes to client deliverable (e.g. Zoom call notes to action plan to first-draft proposal or report)

2        New opportunity qualification and proposal writing (from inbound lead or referral to a sent proposal)

3        Client status report or update (recurring; often the most manual)

4        Research brief for a new client or sector (pre-engagement intelligence gathering)

5        Scoping and briefing a technical build (from client problem to a brief an engineering team can act on)

6        Onboarding a new consultant (what they need to learn and do in their first two weeks)
