Why this document exists

This is the deliverable that turns a good conversation into a next step. It lands while the call is still fresh, it uses the client's own words, and
it ends with exactly one recommendation. Hold the 24-hour line; a map that arrives on day three reads like an afterthought.

Two rules carry over from the J2i standard. Every number comes from the call or reads ESTIMATE NEEDED. And this page goes through a
named human review before it goes anywhere near the client.




Situation summary

[Two or three sentences, no more: what the client does, where they are in their growth, and the core operational challenge as THEY
described it. Use their vocabulary, not consulting language.]


Worked example: "Riverbend Health is an intensive outpatient clinic mid-way through federal credentialing, planning to
launch in August. The front desk handles 40+ calls a day about credentialing status while billing re-keys the same patient
data into two systems. The team's words: 'we are drowning in admin before we have even opened.'"
