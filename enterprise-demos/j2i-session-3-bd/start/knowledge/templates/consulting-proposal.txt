The Problem

Most professional teams are already using AI tools. The problem is that each person uses different tools, in different ways, with no shared
standard. For [Client Name], this creates three specific risks:

Uneven delivery quality. When consultants use AI differently, the quality of outputs varies by person rather than by standard.

Duplicated effort. Tasks that one person has automated, another still does manually. The team is not operating as a unit.

Missed speed. Reports, status updates, research summaries, first-draft proposals: these can be produced in minutes with the right
workflow. Without shared standards, the team is working at half capacity.

The gap is not tools. It is the deliberate, professional use of those tools across the whole team, in a consulting context.




Our Approach

We do not run generic AI training. Every session in this programme is built around how [Client Name] actually works: its delivery processes,
its client relationships, its business development motion, and its team structure.

Before the programme: We send a short discovery questionnaire to understand the team's current AI usage, the workflows that matter
most, and the client-facing contexts where AI will have the highest impact. Session content is adapted from that input.

During the programme: Each session runs live, led by senior practitioners. Participants do not watch demonstrations: they build working
tools themselves, using their own real work as the material. Every session ends with something the team uses from that day forward.
